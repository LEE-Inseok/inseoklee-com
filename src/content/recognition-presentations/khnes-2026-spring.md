## Topic

Formic-acid-promoted SBH hydrolysis with an integrated selective CO → hydrocarbon conversion stage. The selectivity eliminates the need for downstream CO scrubbing and meets PEM fuel-cell purity requirements directly at the reactor outlet.

## Why this venue

KHNES is the primary domestic venue for hydrogen-energy research in Korea — closest audience for the catalysis and fuel-cell purity aspects of the work.
