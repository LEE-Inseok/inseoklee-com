## Format

The Three Minute Thesis (3MT) competition requires distilling months of technical work into a compelling three-minute narrative, supported by a single static slide, accessible to a general audience.

## Significance

Recognized for clear and engaging communication of complex research. 3MT requires the ability to compress technical depth without losing substance — a skill as important as the research itself.
