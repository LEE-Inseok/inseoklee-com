## Tools & Frameworks

- **R** — statistical computing language
- **tidyverse** (dplyr · tidyr · readr) — data wrangling pipelines
- **ggplot2** — layered grammar of graphics for publication-quality plots
- **Statistical modeling** — linear / multivariate regression, ANOVA, hypothesis testing

## How These Tools Show Up in My Research

- CCPP-LOHC manuscript — linear-regression coefficient inspection (Track A) backed by R-style statistical thinking
- Pareto-front and parity plots in manuscript figures — ggplot2 layered approach informs the figure design even when produced via matplotlib/seaborn
- Result interpretation discipline — hypothesis testing and effect-size reasoning carry over to every simulation-study conclusion
